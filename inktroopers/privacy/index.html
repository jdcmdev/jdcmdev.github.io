Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The short version

Ink Troopers does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game doesn’t need an internet connection.

Information we collect

None. Ink Troopers does not collect any personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or usage statistics, and it does not use any third-party services that would.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file on your device containing the battles you’ve reached, your stars, your coins, the troopers you’ve unlocked and upgraded, your chosen squad, which tips you’ve seen, and your sound and vibration settings. This file never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game, or you can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings.

Permissions

Ink Troopers asks for one permission: vibration, so your phone can give a short buzz at big moments in a battle. It involves no data, and you can turn it off in the game’s settings. The game does not request internet access or any other permission.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first, and the date above will change.
