Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version

Hive Whisper does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game doesn’t need an internet connection.

Information we collect

None. Hive Whisper does not collect any personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or usage statistics, and it does not use any third-party services that would.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file on your device containing your stars for each level, your best scores, your daily-meadow streak, your chosen looks in the Garden, and your sound settings. This file never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game, or you can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings.

Permissions

Hive Whisper does not request any special Android permissions, including internet access.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first, and the date above will change.
